How to read the status labels on this site.

Status labels describe the evidence category of a statement. A documented statement is not necessarily an operating service or approved for launch. This review preview contains historical and proposed material awaiting owner review. Online access does not constitute content approval or launch acceptance.

  • Documented

    Supported by a stated source, such as HAKA's strategy documents or an owner confirmation. This does not by itself verify present operations or authorize publication. Original wording and editorial interpretation must be distinguished.

  • Historical proposition

    A proposition or concept from HAKA's historical strategy whose current operating status has not been verified.

  • Proposed

    A new direction under consideration. Not an approved project, partnership or commitment.

  • Active

    Verified current activity with owner approval to publish. None is listed at present.

  • Status pending

    Appears in HAKA assets but its purpose or status has not been confirmed.
