A proposed shared platform for HAKA's sector propositions.

Five capability families form the proposed architecture. None is presented here as verified current delivery capacity. Lifecycle stage and data prerequisites are shown separately from proposal status; each use case would require its own scope, rights and acceptance evidence.

01

Digital twins and simulation

Applications across product engineering, manufacturing systems, industrial processes, energy systems, water infrastructure, agricultural systems, medical technology, defence and other complex systems.

Twin fidelity, data access and model validation must be established for each use case.

Product twin

Proposed

Lifecycle stage
Design
Evidence prerequisites

Lifecycle stage

Proposed

Lifecycle stage
Operation

02

Advanced manufacturing and 3D printing

A proposed shared manufacturing capability for the whole portfolio — not an asset of any one sector.

Proposed
  • Additive manufacturing and design for additive manufacturing
  • Rapid prototyping and functional parts
  • Tooling and fixtures
  • Materials qualification and process selection
  • Post-processing and quality assurance
  • Component redesign and local supplier development

Selecting a manufacturing process

Not every part is suitable for additive manufacturing. Initial process selection considers volume, material, tolerances, geometry and economics; part-specific qualification is still required.

Additive

Low volumes, complex geometry, consolidated parts, fast iteration.

Injection moulding

High volumes of polymer parts once tooling is justified.

Extrusion

Constant cross-sections in long runs — profiles, tubes, channels.

CNC machining

Tight tolerances, metal parts, surface finish, moderate volumes.
