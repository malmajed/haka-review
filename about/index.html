Home/About HAKA

A Saudi company built to develop and localize strategic technologies.

HAKA Digital Company was founded on a documented vision of advanced digital industry — engineering design, analysis, product development and manufacturing preparation — and a mission to lead Saudi and international clients through their technology and industrial strategies.

Our vision

أن تصبح الشركة الرائدة في مجال الصناعة المتقدمة الرقمية ومن أهم جوانبها تصميم وتحليل وتطوير وتحسين المنتجات والإعداد للتصنيع والمشاركة في تنفيذ التحول الاقتصادي والصناعي للمملكة والتوجيه لأبناء وبنات الوطن المتحمسين للمشاركة الفاعلة فيه.

To become the leading company in advanced digital industry — product design, analysis, development, improvement and preparation for manufacturing — contributing to the Kingdom's economic and industrial transformation and guiding the Saudi women and men eager to take part. English rendering of the documented Arabic.

Our mission

قيادة العملاء من المؤسسات الحكومية والشركات الصناعية الوطنية أو الشركات العالمية الراغبة في دخول السوق المحلي من خلال عملية تطوير استراتيجيتهم التقنية والصناعية وتنفيذها وتحسين منتجاتهم المبتكرة.

To lead government institutions, Saudi industrial companies and international companies entering the local market through developing and executing their technology and industrial strategies and improving their innovative products.

02

Methodology

Methodology described in HAKA's strategy

  • Saudi talent first — students and graduates included — expanding globally as needed.
  • A gradual approach, in step with market development in strategic sectors.
  • Maximum value for owners and customers, with strict cost control.
  • Best use of remote work and best-in-class capability from anywhere in the world when needed.
  • The latest digital and engineering technologies — artificial intelligence, blockchain and 3D printing.
Documented service scope

What HAKA set out to provide

  1. Developing specialised strategic plans for targeted industrial and technology sectors.
  2. Participating with domestic and international parties in executing those plans.
  3. Supporting national universities and colleges in taking part.
  4. Securing national capabilities — training centres for design, analysis and systems engineering; virtual rapid-prototyping; virtual industrial incubators; R&D and test support teams; materials and tooling supply; strategic links with leading global firms.
